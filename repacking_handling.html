Repacking & Handling Workflow

01

Material Review

We review material type, packaging condition, quantity, handling needs, and client requirements before starting the work.

02

Work Area Preparation

The work area is arranged for safe movement, stacking, bagging, labeling, loading, and material flow.

03

Repacking & Re-Bagging

Materials are repacked or re-bagged into suitable sacks, jumbo bags, drums, or packaging based on the requested handling method.

04

Labeling & Arrangement

Items are labeled, grouped, arranged, and prepared for warehouse storage, loading, or direct delivery.

05

Loading Support

Our team supports loading, unloading, stacking, and movement of materials using suitable manpower and equipment coordination.

06

Ready for Storage or Transport

Materials are prepared for the next step, whether storage, warehouse handover, site delivery, or transportation.
