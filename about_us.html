About Talia

Local Field Support for Oilfield, Logistics & General Services

TALIA General & Oilfield Services is a Yemen-based company operating from Hadramout, Seiyun. We support companies with transportation, oilfield material supply, vehicle rental, workforce supply, repacking, and practical operational support across Yemen.

Company Overview

TALIA provides reliable support services for companies working in oilfield, industrial, logistics, commercial, and project environments. Our work is built around practical execution, local knowledge, responsive coordination, and safe handling of people, vehicles, materials, and site requirements.
